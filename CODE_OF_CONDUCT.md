# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in this project a welcoming, safe, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and empathetic.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- Respect privacy and never publish personal candidate or contributor data.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention.
- Trolling, sustained disruption, or deliberately misleading contributions.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Using project spaces to facilitate abusive, deceptive, or unlawful activity.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this code. Reports should be sent privately to the maintainers through GitHub. Reports will be reviewed fairly and confidentially to the extent practical.

This code applies in project spaces and when someone is publicly representing the project.
